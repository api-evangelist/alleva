A behavioral health treatment plan is the documented clinical roadmap for a client’s care: the diagnosis, the measurable goals, the interventions tied to named clinicians, and the scheduled reviews that show the plan is current. It’s also one of the first documents a payer or surveyor turns to when reviewing whether care was medically necessary.
